AI is most useful to a marketing team in the repetitive, data-heavy, and foundational places: the scaling work that consumes valuable campaign planning time.

## Where it helps today

- **Campaign variation** — generating targeted ad copy variants and channel-specific snippets instantly.
- **Audience segmentation** — clustering customer data to identify behavioral patterns and trends.
- **Content repurposing** — turning long-form whitepapers or case studies into social media threads and email snippets.
- **Performance reporting** — summarizing multi-channel campaign metrics and pulling key insights from analytics dashboards.

## Where it doesn't

Original brand voice, creative direction, and empathetic audience connection still belong to people. AI speeds up the execution around a campaign, not the core strategic vision itself.

## A sensible way to begin

Pick one repetitive marketing workflow, measure its turnaround time before and after, and keep a person fully responsible for the final output.
