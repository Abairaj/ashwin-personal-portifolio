Eight years in, the lessons that stayed with me are rarely the ones I expected to learn when I started.

## 1. Start before you feel ready

Clarity comes from doing the work, not from planning it.

## 2. Cash flow is the real scoreboard

Revenue is a headline. Cash flow decides whether you are still here next year.

## 3. People first, then process

A good process cannot rescue the wrong team, but the right team will build a good process.

## 4. Write things down

Anything that lives only in the founder's head is a risk to the business.

## 5. Say no more often

Every yes is paid for with attention you no longer have for something else.

## 6. Build systems, not heroics

If it only works when you are in the room, it does not work yet.

## 7. Reputation compounds

How you close a difficult project is remembered longer than how you won it.

## 8. Stay a student

The market changes faster than any one person's experience.
