When a business stalls, the first instinct is to look for more leads, more marketing, a new product. In most cases the constraint is somewhere less exciting.

## The problem is usually inside

Growth exposes whatever is weak: unclear ownership, slow decisions, work that depends on one person remembering to do it.

## Three questions worth asking

1. **Systems** — what happens to an enquiry, an order or a complaint when nobody is watching?
2. **Execution** — how long does a decision take to become an action?
3. **People** — who owns the outcome, by name?

## Fix the engine before adding fuel

More demand poured into a leaking system produces more leaks. Tighten the system first, and growth tends to follow.
