AI is most useful to a business in the unglamorous places: the repetitive, written, rule-heavy work that slows every team down.

## Where it helps today

- **Documentation** — turning meetings, site visits and calls into structured notes.
- **Customer communication** — drafting first responses and follow-ups.
- **Estimation and reporting** — summarising numbers that already exist in spreadsheets.
- **Internal knowledge** — answering "how do we do this here?" from your own documents.

## Where it doesn't

Judgement, relationships and accountability still belong to people. AI speeds up the work around a decision, not the decision itself.

## A sensible way to begin

Pick one process, measure it before and after, and keep a person responsible for the result.
