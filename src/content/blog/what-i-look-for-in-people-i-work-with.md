Skills can be taught. The traits below are much harder to add later, so I look for them first.

## Ownership

People who treat a problem as theirs until it is solved, not until it is handed over.

## Honesty about bad news

I would rather hear about a delay on day two than on the deadline.

## Curiosity

The people who ask "why do we do it this way?" are usually the ones who improve it.

## Consistency

Showing up the same way on an ordinary Tuesday as on launch day.

## Respect

For clients, for colleagues, and for the people on site whose work makes everything else possible.
